What this is

An agentic data-operations control layer over a lending data estate: 6 upstream services, 6 governed Spark pipelines, and a full incident lifecycle from an automatically detected trust failure to a verified, reviewable repair. A health monitor watches every data product, diagnoses a break, and proposes a bounded fix for you to accept or reject -- nothing is ever promoted without that explicit decision. The Q&A tab is one entry point into this same system, not a separate chatbot.

Ask a business question
