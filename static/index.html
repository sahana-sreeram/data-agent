What this is

An agentic data-operations control layer over a lending data estate: 6 upstream services, 6 governed Spark pipelines, mostly-automatic context enrichment with a human-approved semantic layer, and a full incident lifecycle from business signal to verified, reviewable repair. Natural-language Q&A (Incidents tab) is one entry point into this system, not the whole of it.
